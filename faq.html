Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before booking. Tap a question to expand it.

Is this a real salon?

Not in the traditional sense. Hair Weaving Solutions is a private studio, one client at a time, either at the studio or in your own home. There's no waiting room and no walk-ins.

Which areas do you serve?

The studio is in Bridgeport, CT, and Danielle welcomes clients from across Fairfield and New Haven counties in Connecticut and Westchester County, New York. Some services can be done in your home, depending on the service and location; just note your preference when booking.

What's the difference between a Traditional and a Versatile sew-in?

A Traditional sew-in leaves out a small amount of your natural hair for seamless blending around the hairline and part. A Versatile sew-in leaves little to none out, giving you maximum styling flexibility and a fuller, more luxurious finish. In your consultation, Danielle helps you choose the one that fits your hair and your lifestyle.

How much does a sew-in cost?

There's no flat price, because no two installs are the same. What you invest depends on the service you choose, the length and fullness you're after, and whether you're sourcing your own hair or Danielle is sourcing it for you. You'll have a clear figure before anything is booked; it's discussed privately in your consultation, along with what maintenance between installs looks like.

Will a sew-in damage my hair?

Not when it's installed and cared for properly; that's the whole point of a healthy-hair-first approach. Danielle installs with your edges, scalp, and long-term growth in mind, and the natural hair underneath is protected while it rests. Following the care guidance between appointments keeps it healthy.

Do you provide the hair, or do I need to source my own?

Both options are available. This will be part of your intake questions.

How long does a sew-in last?

Most sew-ins are worn for about six to eight weeks before they're taken down and reinstalled, usually with a maintenance visit partway through to refresh the leave-out and care for your scalp. Leaving an install in much longer than that is where damage tends to start, so Danielle sets the right window for your hair at your appointment rather than leaving it open-ended.

How do I care for my sew-in between appointments?

Keep your scalp and the leave-out clean and moisturized, be gentle around your edges, and wrap or protect your hair at night. Danielle will give you guidance tailored to your install and let you know when it's time to come back for maintenance.

Do you offer wigs, closures, or silk presses?

Yes. While the studio specializes in natural-looking Traditional and Versatile sew-ins, lace wigs, closures, and silk press services are also available by consultation. Mention what you're looking for when you reach out.

Do you come to my home?

Yes, depending on the service and location. Let us know your preference when booking.

How long does an appointment take?

This varies by service; your confirmation email will include an estimated time.

What if I'm not sure exactly what I want?

That's completely normal. Every appointment starts with a consultation to talk through your goals before anything is decided.

Do you accept walk-ins?

No, all appointments are by booking only, to protect the private, one-on-one experience.
