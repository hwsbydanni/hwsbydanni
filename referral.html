Referral Program

Share the Studio, Share the Love

A simple thank-you for both of you.

Know someone who would enjoy a private, one-on-one hair experience? Refer them to Hair Weaving Solutions by Danni, and once they complete their first paid appointment, there will be a special thank-you for both of you.

For You

A $10 Referral Credit

For every new client you refer who completes and pays for their first appointment, you will receive a $10 referral credit toward your next qualifying service.

For Your Friend

A Complimentary Welcome Gift

Your referred friend will receive one complimentary surprise gift at the completion of their first paid appointment. Gifts are personally selected by Danni and may include a hair-care or beauty item. Selection varies and is available while supplies last.

How It Works
  1. Share the HWS by Danni website or booking link with your friend.
  2. When booking, your friend must enter the full first and last name of the existing client who referred them.
  3. After the new client completes and pays for their first appointment, the referring client will receive a $10 referral credit.
  4. The new client will receive their complimentary surprise gift at the end of their first completed appointment.
Program Details
  • The referred guest must be a first-time HWS by Danni client.
  • Referral credit is issued only after the new client completes and pays for their first appointment. Canceled appointments and no-shows do not qualify.
  • One $10 credit is earned for each qualifying paid referral.
  • Only one $10 referral credit may be redeemed per appointment.
  • Credits must be used within 90 days of being issued.
  • Referral credits cannot be applied toward deposits, hair purchases, retail products, or previous balances.
  • Credits cannot be transferred, combined with other promotions, or exchanged for cash.
  • Complimentary gifts are selected by Danni, may vary, and have no cash or exchange value.
  • HWS by Danni reserves the right to modify or discontinue the referral program at any time.

No public posting is required — simply have your friend provide your full name when booking.
