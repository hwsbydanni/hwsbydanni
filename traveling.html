Traveling Services

For clients who would rather not travel to the studio, Danni brings the appointment to you. The same one-on-one install, in your own space.

Danni comes to you

Most appointments happen at the private studio in Bridgeport. Some do not. If getting there is the thing standing between you and the appointment — distance, childcare, a wedding morning, a schedule that will not bend — Danni travels to you.

Nothing about the service changes. It is still one client at a time, still unhurried, still the same work. Only the address is different.

What it covers

A traveling appointment is booked as a single block and can be used for a fresh sew-in, a wig install, or a silk press for a special occasion.

  • Traveling Hairstylist — 8 hours, $400
  • Within a 52-mile radius of Bridgeport, Connecticut
  • One client, in your own home or venue

The 52-mile radius covers most of Fairfield and New Haven Counties and reaches into Westchester County, New York. If you are close to the edge of it, ask before booking.

What is needed at your location

A sew-in takes hours and needs a workable setup: somewhere to sit with good light, a power outlet, and access to water for washing.

The appointment is booked for 8 hours, which is longer than the same service in the studio. Setting up and packing down in an unfamiliar space is not instant, and the block allows for it.

Common Questions

How far will Danni travel?

Within a 52-mile radius of Bridgeport, Connecticut. That covers most of Fairfield and New Haven Counties and reaches into Westchester County, New York.

What does it cost?

The traveling appointment is $400 for an 8-hour block. Pricing is confirmed on the booking page when you select the service.

What can I book it for?

A fresh sew-in, a wig install, or a silk press. If you want something else, ask first.

Why is it booked for 8 hours?

Because the same install takes longer outside the studio. Everything has to be carried in, set up, and packed down, and the block is sized so the appointment is not rushed.

Can Danni travel for a wedding morning?

Yes, and there is a bridal service built for exactly that. The Concierge Bridal Experience includes on-location wedding-day styling and early-morning availability.

Do the usual booking policies apply?

Yes. The $50 deposit, the 72-hour reschedule window and the lateness terms are the same as any appointment, and they are on the policies page.
