Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Last updated: August 16, 2026.

Overview

Hair Weaving Solutions by Danielle ("we," "us," or "HWS") respects your privacy. This policy describes the information we collect through our website and services, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using this site or contacting us, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact or waitlist forms, or book an appointment, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and details about your hair, goals, and timeline. You provide this voluntarily so we can respond and serve you.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we collect basic usage data such as pages viewed, general location, device and browser type, and how you found the site. This is collected through analytics tools (see "Cookies & Analytics" below).

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your inquiries, schedule and manage appointments, provide our services, improve the website, and — only if you have opted in — send occasional updates or offers. We do not use your information for any purpose unrelated to serving you.

Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Analytics tools may set cookies or similar identifiers and collect anonymized usage data. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings; the site will still work without them.

Advertising

We may promote our services through ads on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. If you reached this site through a social media ad, that platform collects information under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. We encourage you to review the privacy settings of any platform you use.

How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed to run our business — for example, with trusted service providers who help us operate, such as our scheduling tool (Acuity Scheduling), our website host, our analytics provider, and, if you opt in to updates, our email service provider. These providers may only use your information to perform services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data Retention & Security

We keep your information only as long as needed to serve you and for reasonable business and legal purposes, then delete it. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices & Rights

You can opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any message. You may also ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us through our Contact page and we'll respond promptly.

Children's Privacy

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach out any time through our Contact page and Danielle will be glad to help.

This policy applies to Hair Weaving Solutions by Danielle and this website only.
