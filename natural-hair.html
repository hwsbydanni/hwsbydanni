Why book with Danni

Twenty-seven years installing hair, in a private studio where you are the only client in the room. No waiting area, no double-booking, no one working on someone else while your hair sits. The appointment you book is the appointment you get.

Danni’s whole practice is built on healthy hair first — protective styling that lets your own hair grow underneath rather than paying for length with damage. These services are that principle on its own, without an install on top.

A $50 deposit holds your slot and comes off the balance — it is not an extra charge. Because every appointment is one-on-one, there are only so many in a week, and the calendar fills furthest out first. If you see a date that works, take it.
