Your First Visit

What to expect, step by step

Danielle takes a limited number of new clients and gives each one her full attention. Here's how your first experience unfolds.

1

Reach out

Start with a short consultation request, or reserve directly. Danielle personally reviews every inquiry to make sure it's the right fit — for both of you.

2

Your consultation

Together you talk through your hair, your goals, and the right install — Traditional or Versatile. No guesswork, and never any pressure.

3

Your private appointment

One-on-one in the studio, or your home for select services. No waiting room, no crowd, no rush — just careful, focused work.

4

Aftercare & rebooking

You leave with guidance to keep your hair healthy, and a simple plan for when to return for maintenance. Returning clients enjoy priority rebooking and standing appointments.

Your privacy, protected. Your appointment and your details stay between you and Danielle — never shared or posted without your permission.
