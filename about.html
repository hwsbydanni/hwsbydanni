About

Meet Danielle

The story, philosophy, and experience behind HWS.

Healthy hair first — then the length, the fullness, and a finish that looks like it grew from your own scalp.

With more than 27 years of experience, Danielle — known to her clients as Danni — is the stylist and owner behind Hair Weaving Solutions by Danni. She specializes in natural-looking Traditional and Versatile sew-ins, custom extension work, and protective styling designed to enhance each client's beauty while protecting the health of their natural hair.

Her approach begins with the health of your own hair. Extensions should enhance what's yours, never compromise it, so every install is done with care for your edges, your scalp, and your long-term growth.

Her private Bridgeport, Connecticut studio offers a peaceful, one-on-one experience where every appointment is personalized, discreet, and never rushed — no crowded salon floor, no overlapping chairs, no watching the clock.

That attention to detail, advanced technique, and commitment to exceptional service have earned the trust of busy professionals, public figures, and women who value beautiful hair and a more elevated salon experience. Clients come from across Fairfield and New Haven counties in Connecticut and Westchester County, New York — many of whom, once they find her, won't let anyone else touch their hair.

Whether you're visiting the private studio or booking a travel appointment, Danni's goal is simple: seamless, sophisticated results that look natural, feel secure, and leave you feeling like the best version of yourself.
