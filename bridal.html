Hair that holds all day, and photographs well

A wedding is a long day under hot lights, in and out of cars, hugged by everyone you know. Hair that looked right at nine in the morning has to still look right at nine at night, and photograph well in between.

Danni is a bridal hairstylist based in Bridgeport, Connecticut, with 27 years behind the chair. She styles brides and special occasion clients one at a time in her private studio, and travels to wedding venues across Fairfield and New Haven Counties and Westchester County, NY.

That is a different brief from an ordinary appointment, and Danni offers two ways to meet it: one for brides who want their hair done before the day, and one for brides who want her there on the morning itself.

Signature Bridal Install — 4 hours, $250

For brides who prefer to have their hair completed before the wedding day.

  • Hair installation
  • Bridal style consultation — required before booking
  • Wedding-ready styling
  • Pin curls for lasting results
  • Hair care instructions

Hair extensions are not included, and this service does not cover wedding-day styling or on-location work. If you want Danni with you on the morning, the Concierge experience below is the one.

Common Questions

Which one do I need?

If you want your hair finished in advance and you will handle the morning yourself, the Signature install at $250. If you want Danni at your venue on the day, doing your hair and your veil and watching the timeline, the Concierge experience from $650.

Is a consultation required?

Yes. A bridal style consultation is required before booking the Signature install, and a consultation and preview appointment are both included in the Concierge experience.

Is hair included?

No. Extensions are not included in either service. Danni sells a vendor list if you want guidance on where to buy.

How does the travel fee work?

The Concierge experience starts at $650 and the travel fee is based on your location. On-location work runs within a 52-mile radius of Bridgeport — see traveling services.

What is the Bridal Agreement?

A signed agreement is required to reserve your wedding date for the Concierge experience. It is what takes the date off the calendar for everyone else.

Can I have a sew-in installed before the wedding instead?

Yes, and many brides do. A traditional or versatile sew-in installed ahead of time, then styled for the day, is a common route. Raise it at your consultation so the timing works.
