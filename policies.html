Studio Policies

Booking & Studio Policies

A private, one-on-one studio runs a little differently. These policies keep every appointment unhurried and fair to each client.

Appointment Schedule

Appointments are by booking only, offered in set blocks. Reserve yours on the booking page.

  • Sunday8:00 AM · 12:00 PM

Deposits & Payment

A nonrefundable $50 deposit is required to secure every appointment and is due at the time of booking. The deposit is applied toward the total cost of the service.

Your remaining balance is due on the day of service, by cash or card. If you are paying in cash, please bring exact change.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

If you need to cancel or move an appointment, starting that process is your responsibility — please rebook through your booking link rather than waiting to hear from the studio.

To carry your deposit over to a new date, the change must be made at least 72 hours before your appointment. A late cancellation — any change made inside that window — forfeits the deposit, and a new one is required to book again.

If no standard appointment is available, you may request a squeeze-in appointment. Squeeze-ins are not guaranteed, require approval, and are priced at double the regular service rate.

Late Arrival

Because every appointment is private and one-on-one with no buffer between clients, arriving late shortens the time available for your service.

A 15-minute grace period is provided. Arrivals beyond 15 minutes may require the appointment to be shortened, rescheduled, or canceled at Danni's discretion. If the appointment cannot be completed, the deposit is forfeited.

Arrival Preparation

Clients must arrive with their natural hair clean, completely dry, thoroughly detangled, and free of glue, thread, residue, or excessive product — unless a shampoo service is included in the appointment.

Danni does not remove, repair, maintain, or work over an installation completed by another stylist. Any existing installation must be removed before the appointment, unless removal has been booked as an additional service.

Missed Appointments

A valid card on file is required to book. An appointment missed without any notice is a no-show, charged at 100% of the scheduled service price to the card on file. Each private appointment reserves a dedicated block of time that cannot ordinarily be filled at the last minute.

If the no-show charge cannot be collected, the outstanding balance must be paid in full before another appointment can be booked. If something goes wrong on the day, get in touch — the policy exists for silence, not for emergencies.

Age Requirement

HWS primarily serves clients age 18 and older. Any minor accepted for service must have prior approval, and a parent or legal guardian must complete the booking and accept financial responsibility.

Guests & Companions

Because HWS is a private, one-on-one studio, additional guests and children are not permitted unless they are the client receiving service or prior approval has been granted for a parent or legal guardian accompanying a minor. This applies to both studio and home-visit appointments.

Redo & Correction

Any concerns must be reported within 72 hours of the completed appointment. Corrections are determined at Danni's discretion and must relate directly to the original service. Refunds are not provided. This covers genuine service concerns, not a change of mind about a style completed as requested.

Hair Sourcing & Client-Provided Hair

If Danni sources the hair, requests require a minimum of 10 business days' notice. Availability is not guaranteed until the hair has been confirmed and payment has been received. The cost of sourced hair is confirmed during your consultation.

If the client provides their own hair, Danni is not responsible for its quality, durability, or outcome, and it should be brought clean and ready at the appointment.

Allergies & Scalp Sensitivities

Clients are asked to disclose any known allergies, scalp sensitivities, or reactions to hair products at booking or during intake. Danni reserves the right to decline or modify a service if a disclosed condition makes it unsafe.

Questions about any of these before booking? Reach out any time — Danni is happy to walk through them with you.
