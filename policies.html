Studio Policies

Booking & Studio Policies

A private, one-on-one studio runs a little differently. These policies keep every appointment unhurried and fair to each client.

Deposits & Payment

A deposit is required to secure any appointment and is due at the time of booking. All deposits are non-refundable. Every deposit is deducted from your total balance, so it is part of the cost of your service rather than an extra charge on top of it.

Your remaining balance is due on the day of service, by cash or card. If you are paying in cash, please bring exact change.

Rescheduling & Cancellations

If you need to cancel or move an appointment, starting that process is your responsibility — please rebook through your booking link rather than waiting to hear from the studio.

To carry your deposit over to a new date, the change must be made at least 72 hours before your appointment. Changes made inside that window may mean forfeiting the deposit, and a new one is required to book again.

If nothing suitable is open through the booking link, a squeeze-in appointment is the remaining option. Squeeze-ins are fitted outside the normal schedule and are charged at double the regular installation price.

Late Arrival

Because every appointment is private and one-on-one with no buffer between clients, arriving late shortens the time available for your service.

There is a 15-minute grace period. Arriving more than 20 minutes late means the appointment is canceled, as there is no longer time to complete the work properly.

Missed Appointments

An appointment missed without any notice is charged at 100% of the service price, and the client will not be able to book with the studio again. A single private appointment fills the whole of that day’s slot, so a no-show cannot be given to anyone else.

If something goes wrong on the day, get in touch — the policy exists for silence, not for emergencies.

Guests & Companions

Appointments are for the booked client only. Guests, including children, are asked not to accompany clients to appointments unless discussed and approved in advance. This applies to both studio and home-visit appointments.

Redo & Correction

If a client isn't happy with their result, they're asked to reach out within a few days of the appointment. Danielle will review the concern and, where appropriate, offer a complimentary adjustment. This covers genuine service concerns, not a change of mind about a style completed as requested.

Hair Sourcing & Client-Provided Hair

If Danielle sources the hair, it is ordered ahead of the appointment, so clients should confirm their service and hair needs well in advance. The cost of sourced hair is confirmed during your consultation.

If the client provides their own hair, Danielle is not responsible for its quality, durability, or outcome, and it should be brought clean and ready at the appointment.

Patch Test & Allergy Disclosure

Clients are asked to disclose any known allergies, scalp sensitivities, or reactions to hair products at booking or during intake. Danielle reserves the right to decline or modify a service if a disclosed condition makes it unsafe.

Questions about any of these before booking? Reach out any time — Danielle is happy to walk through them with you.
